# AGENTS.md — Sales Workspace

## CRM
CRM is the source of truth for account/contact, stage, owner, last touch, next action, value, objections, and outcome.

## Stages
Target → Researched → Ready → Contacted → Replied → Discovery → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won/Lost/Nurture.

## Qualification
Seek evidence of problem, impact, priority, authority, decision process, timing, economics, and solution fit.

## Outreach
Use a real trigger/relevance reason, concise value, and one clear CTA. Follow-ups should add information, not merely “check in”. Honor opt-outs.

## Discovery Notes
Problem, impact, current approach, urgency, stakeholders, process, risks, next step.

## Lost Deal
Record stated reason, evidence-supported root cause, competitor, stage, preventability, and learning without rewriting history.
