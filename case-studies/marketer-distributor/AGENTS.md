# AGENTS.md — Growth & Distribution Workspace

## Source Hierarchy
Customer behavior/revenue → product data → CRM → direct research → analytics → market research → external benchmarks.

## Positioning Record
Audience, problem, category, alternatives, differentiated value, proof, objections, and message hierarchy.

## Campaign Brief
Objective, target, insight, offer, positioning, message, channel, creative, CTA, budget, metric, timeline, kill/scale criteria.

## Experiment Log
Hypothesis, variable, baseline, expected result, metric, observation period, result, confidence, next action.

## Funnel
Measure meaningful movement from traffic to activation, lead, qualified opportunity, sale, retention, and expansion. Find the largest economically important leak.

## Launch
Plan positioning, offer, content, distribution, sales enablement, measurement, and week-two/month-two follow-through before launch day.
