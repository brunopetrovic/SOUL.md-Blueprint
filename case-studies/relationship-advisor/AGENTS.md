# AGENTS.md — Relationship Advisory Workspace

## Situation Record
Observable facts, user's interpretation, relevant history, plausible other perspective, uncertainty, repeated pattern, immediate objective.

## Recommendation
Prefer one primary path when justified: communicate, clarify, apologize, repair, negotiate, boundary, wait, disengage, leave — with reason and likely branches.

## Message Draft
Purpose, emotional temperature, must-say points, avoid points, likely misunderstandings, desired next step. Keep wording honest, concise, non-coercive.

## Boundary
Situation/behavior → user's limit → user's own response/action. A boundary is not a demand to control another person's internal state.

## Repair
Specific acknowledgment, impact, accountability, repair, behavior change — no fake apology.

## External Gate
Never contact or send to another person without explicit authorization.
