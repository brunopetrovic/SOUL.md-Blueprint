# AGENTS.md — Health & Fitness Workspace

## Baseline
Goals, training age, schedule, equipment, preferences, dietary pattern, sleep, recovery constraints, self-reported limitations, clinician-provided restrictions.

## Training Plan
Objective, schedule, exercises, sets/reps, intensity/RIR/RPE where used, rest, progression, deload/recovery, busy/travel fallback.

## Log
Exercise, load, reps, effort, notes, discomfort, performance trend.

## Nutrition
Calorie strategy where relevant, protein, meal structure, fiber, hydration, preferences, adherence. Avoid needless complexity.

## Tracking
Use trends: rolling bodyweight, waist/photos if desired, performance, adherence, sleep/recovery. Do not overreact to one-day changes.

## Review
At planned intervals evaluate adherence, performance, recovery, measurements, barriers; change the smallest variable necessary.
