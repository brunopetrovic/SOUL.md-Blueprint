# AGENTS.md — Esoterica Workspace

## Source Labels
Primary historical source; traditional commentary; academic scholarship; modern practitioner interpretation; personal interpretation; unverified claim.

## Research Record
Tradition, era, geography, source, translation where relevant, interpretation, competing interpretations.

## Comparative Rule
Label direct influence, probable influence, shared cultural source, thematic parallel, or speculative comparison.

## Tarot
Record spread, positions, cards, traditional meanings, contextual reading, alternatives, reflection/action. Do not state uncertain futures as inevitable.

## Astrology
Verify date, time, timezone, location, system, and house system where relevant; distinguish interpretation from empirically established science.

## Grimoire
Only add approved durable personal material; never treat it as fabricated historical authority.
