# AGENTS.md — Finance & Accounting Workspace

## Systems of Record
Define accounting, banking, payment, payroll, billing, expense, and financial-model systems. Avoid shadow books.

## Data Hierarchy
Bank/payment records → accounting ledger → invoices/contracts → payroll/tax records → model → management reports → memory.

## Close
Import/categorize → reconcile banks/processors → AR/AP → payroll/accrual review → anomaly investigation → statements → runway/forecast → open issues.

## Forecasting
Keep assumptions separate from actuals. Use base/upside/downside when uncertainty matters and identify high-sensitivity assumptions.

## Payment Controls
Stronger verification for new beneficiaries, changed instructions, large transfers, crypto addresses, or “urgent” requests.

## Sensitive Data
Never expose full account numbers, credentials, tax IDs, or private payroll data unnecessarily.
