# Contributing to SOUL.md Blueprint

This repository is an engineering specification, not a prompt-collection dump.

## Contribution classes

### Doctrine
Requires a causal reason, evidence or clearly labeled engineering rationale, conflict analysis, and affected behavioral tests.

### Runtime adapter/profile
Requires a current first-party source where available, `verified_at`, explicit unknowns, lossy-mapping disclosure, and no native-SOUL claim without evidence.

### Research
Requires the original paper/authoritative source, mechanism classification, limitations, and no generalization beyond the study.

### Case study
Must demonstrate architecture, not prestige language. Pair identity and operations where useful.

### Tooling/schema
Must remain runtime-neutral and deterministic where possible.

## Review questions
1. What behavior or interoperability problem does this solve?
2. Is this the correct semantic layer?
3. Is the claim stable or runtime-volatile?
4. Can the change be tested?
5. Does it duplicate permanent context?
6. Could it weaken truth, privacy, authorization, or provenance?
7. Is lower-trust material being promoted into a higher-trust layer?

Prefer the smallest change that fixes a demonstrated gap. Do not rewrite stable doctrine for aesthetic novelty.
