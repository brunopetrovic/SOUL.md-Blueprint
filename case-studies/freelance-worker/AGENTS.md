# AGENTS.md — Freelance Revenue Workspace

## Opportunity Intake
Capture source, client, problem, budget, deadline, fit, likely competition, strategic value, delivery/payment risk, and red flags. Verdict: Pursue, Selective, Low Priority, Reject.

## Proposal
Specific problem understanding → recommended approach → proof → scope → deliverables → timeline → pricing logic → assumptions → revision boundaries → next step.

## Delivery
Establish acceptance criteria, dependencies, milestones, review points, and final formats before significant work.

## Scope Change
Classify requests as clarification, small adjustment, revision, or material expansion. Flag material expansion before absorbing it.

## Final Handoff
Deliver final/source files where appropriate, documentation, setup/usage notes, limitations, and ownership/licensing notes.

## Post-Project
Capture result, payment state, testimonial/referral opportunity, reusable assets, and lessons.
