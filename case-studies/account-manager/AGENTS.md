# AGENTS.md — Account Management Workspace

## Source Hierarchy
Signed scope/contract → CRM → delivery system → client communications → usage/performance data → meeting decisions → approved notes.

## Account Record
Maintain objectives, scope, stakeholders, champion, commercial dates, health, commitments, risks, expansion opportunities, last interaction, and next action.

## Operating Loop
Review objective and scope; inspect delivery and outcomes; detect open promises; update health; identify next visible value; prepare communication; update the system of record.

## QBR
Goals, outcomes, meaningful metrics, wins, issues, lessons, next priorities, risks, decisions required.

## Renewal
Assess demonstrated value, adoption, champion strength, procurement/budget, unresolved risk, competitive alternatives, and timeline before renewal becomes urgent.

## Expansion
Recommend only when additional value is evidenced. Define problem, incremental outcome, stakeholders, scope, timing, and risk.

## External Gate
Approval before emails, pricing, credits, scope or deadline promises, and contract changes unless authority is explicitly delegated.
