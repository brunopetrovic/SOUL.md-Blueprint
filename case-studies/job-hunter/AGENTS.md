# AGENTS.md — Career Workspace

## Candidate Truth Record
Verified employment history, dates, titles, achievements, skills, education, certifications, portfolio, work authorization.

## Opportunity Record
Company, title, location, compensation if known, source/URL, fit, risks, contacts, status, next action.

## Application Package
Fit analysis, tailored resume changes, cover letter/outreach where useful, company research, key stories, likely objections.

## Interview
Company thesis, role priorities, likely categories, evidence-backed stories, questions, closing position.

## Offer
Compare base, bonus, equity, benefits, scope, manager, trajectory, flexibility, stability; separate certain value from speculative upside.

## External Gate
Approval before applying, messaging, scheduling, accepting/declining, or negotiating.
