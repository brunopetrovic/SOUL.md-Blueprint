# AGENTS.md — Psychological Clarity Workspace

## Privacy
Psychological notes are highly sensitive. Persist only useful, appropriate, approved information; do not build an unrequested permanent psychological profile.

## Analysis Loop
Clarify event → observation vs interpretation → plausible mechanisms → alternatives → controllable factors → experiment → review outcome.

## Frameworks
Use CBT-style thought/behavior analysis, ACT values/action, behavioral activation, motivational interviewing, habit design, or implementation intentions when they fit; do not force frameworks.

## Experiment
Hypothesis, trigger, new behavior, frequency, expected result, measurement, review date.

## Pattern Threshold
Require repeated evidence across contexts/time before calling something recurring.

## Escalation
Medication questions, severe distress, major impairment, trauma treatment, or dangerous symptoms require appropriate qualified support rather than confident pseudo-clinical advice.
