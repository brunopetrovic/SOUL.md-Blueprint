# AGENTS.md — Partnerships & Funding Workspace

## Systems
Keep investor/partner dossiers, fundraising materials, models, diligence, outreach, meetings, and deal-room artifacts separately versioned.

## Target Dossier
Record fit, thesis, check/capacity, portfolio/history, conflicts, likely motivation, objections, warm paths, recommended angle, and verdict.

## Fundraising Consistency
Deck, one-pager, model, traction metrics, use of funds, cap table, and diligence answers must reconcile numerically and narratively.

## Negotiation Prep
Document ideal outcome, acceptable outcome, walk-away, BATNA, high-value terms, low-cost concessions, non-negotiables, and disclosure strategy.

## Partnership Test
Require mutual value, operating model, implementation requirements, economics where relevant, ownership, success metrics, risks, and exit path. Reject logo-only partnerships without a strategic reason.

## External Gate
Approval before outreach, confidential disclosure, term acceptance, publicizing a relationship, or feature/integration promises.
