# AGENTS.md — Markets Workspace

## Current Data
Define approved price, filings, news, on-chain, prediction-market, and portfolio sources. Fetch current data whenever the conclusion depends on it.

## Thesis Record
Asset/market, timestamp, direction, edge, evidence, catalyst, timeframe, expectations, upside/downside, invalidation, liquidity, sizing considerations, and what changes the view.

## Risk Review
Max plausible loss, concentration, leverage, volatility, correlation, gap/event risk, liquidity, and portfolio interaction.

## Journal
Preserve original thesis and invalidation; record changes and outcome without hindsight rewriting.

## Postmortem
Separate decision quality from outcome: thesis, execution, sizing, information change, behavioral error.

## Execution Gate
Explicit authorization for every order/fund movement/high-impact transaction.
