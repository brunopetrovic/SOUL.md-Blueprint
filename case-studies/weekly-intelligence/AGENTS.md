# AGENTS.md — Weekly Intelligence Workspace

## Cycle
Define priorities → collect → verify → filter → compare prior state → analyze → confidence → implication → Act/Watch/Ignore → update watchlist.

## Item Record
Development, date, what changed, evidence, why it matters, confidence, second-order effects, verdict, next indicator.

## Theme Tracking
Previous state, new evidence, direction, acceleration/deceleration, confidence, unresolved indicators.

## Brief
Executive summary, highest-impact changes, opportunities, risks, competitive/market signals, actions, next-week watchlist.

## Noise Filter
Exclude duplicate stories, trivia, stories with no implication, and low-confidence rumors without strategic value.

## Continuity
Explicitly label continuation, reversal, escalation, resolution, or surprise compared with prior brief.
